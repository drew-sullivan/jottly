Frequently asked

What can I play?

Jottly Favorites include Lightning, Cowpoke, Classic, Shapeshifter, and other deduction games. Popular & Featured contains community creations. Your Games contains games you built, saved, or received from someone else.

Do I need to learn every game first?

No. Every game opens with its own rules sheet and a concrete scoring example. Tap Rules during a game whenever you want to see it again.

How do I play with a friend?

Tap Invite a Friend, choose a game, and enter the secret word that game requires. Your friend opens the invitation, chooses their word, and the match begins. Turns can happen whenever each person has time.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Tap Play Solo and choose from recent games, Jottly Favorites, community games, and Your Games. Games already available on your device can be played offline.

How do I build a game?

Open Remix, choose an existing game as a template, and change its rules. The preview updates as you work. Tap Try It to playtest, then keep and name the versions you enjoy.

Can I share a game without starting a match?

Yes. Open the game in Remix and choose Share Game. The recipient gets the exact rules and can play it solo, save it, share it, or remix it again.

How do I save a game someone shared?

After playing, Jottly offers to save the game to Your Games if it is not already there. You can also open the completed game later and save it from there. Original creator attribution stays attached.

How do I edit or delete one of my games?

Open Remix, select the game under Your Games, then use its menu to edit, share, remix, or delete it. Editing changes that game; remixing creates a new one.

I'm not getting turn notifications.

Make sure notifications are enabled for Jottly in iOS Settings → Notifications → Jottly.

Is Jottly really free?

Completely. No ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or sign-up.

How do I remove a game or my data?

Leave or remove an active game from Home. Delete a saved game from its menu in Remix. There is no Jottly account; see the Privacy Policy for local, iCloud, shared-game, and anonymous analytics retention details.
