Play. Build. Share.

Jottly is a home for word and letter-deduction games. Start with a favorite, discover something new, or build a game nobody has played before.

Play

Pick a game that fits your brain.

Every Jottly game is its own bundle of rules. Some reveal green and yellow tiles. Some give only a number. Some move the secret word after every guess. Each one begins with a clear rules sheet, then gets out of your way.

Play a quick solo game, challenge a friend on your own schedule, or open an exact game someone shared with you.

Lightning

Familiar green and yellow feedback for every letter. Fast, direct, and easy to jump into.

Cowpoke

Learn how many letters match and how many are perfectly placed, but not which ones.

Classic

One score tells you how many letters match. Every useful deduction is yours to make.

Shapeshifter

Green and yellow clues meet a moving target that keeps honoring everything you have learned.

Build

Start familiar. Make it yours.

Remix turns every game into a starting point. Change the word length, clue style, target behavior, hints, constraints, abilities, and ending rules. The preview changes with your choices, and Try It launches the real game immediately.

Those choices combine into millions of possible games, from tiny two-letter experiments to deep deduction challenges. Keep the ones that are fun, give them a name and icon, then edit or remix them whenever inspiration strikes.

What kind of games are in Jottly?

Jottly Favorites include Lightning, Cowpoke, Classic, Shapeshifter, and other deduction games. Popular & Featured adds community creations, and Your Games holds games you build or save.

Do I need to learn every ruleset?

No. Every game opens with its own rules sheet and a concrete scoring example. During play, tap Rules whenever you want a refresher.

How does Remix work?

Choose any game as a template, change its rules, and tap Try It. Keep the games you enjoy, then name, edit, share, or remix them again.

How do I play with a friend?

Tap Invite a Friend, choose a game, and enter the secret word that its rules require. You can also share a reusable game without starting a match.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Jottly Favorites, community games, your own games, and games shared with you can all be played solo. Available solo games keep working offline.

Can I save or remix a shared game?

Yes. Open the shared game, play it, and save it to Your Games. Its original attribution stays with it, and you can share it onward or create a new remix.

I’m not getting turn notifications.

Enable notifications for Jottly in iOS Settings → Notifications → Jottly.

Is Jottly really free?

Completely. No ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or sign-up.

How do I manage a game I created?

Open Remix, choose a game from Your Games, then use its menu to edit, share, remix, or delete it.
